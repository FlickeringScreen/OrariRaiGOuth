Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 6, 2024

1. Introduction

RAI Shift Calendar Generator ("we", "our", or "the app") is a client-side web application that helps users convert PDF shift schedules into calendar files. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers.

The app processes the following data entirely within your browser:

  • PDF Files: Your uploaded shift schedule PDF is processed locally in your browser and never sent to any server
  • Personal Information: Your name (first and last) is used only to filter your shifts from the PDF and label the calendar file
  • Local Storage: Your name may be saved in your browser's local storage for convenience on future visits

3. Google Calendar Integration

When you choose to use the "Add to Google Calendar" feature:

  • You will be asked to sign in with your Google account via Google's OAuth 2.0 system
  • We request permission to add events to your Google Calendar (scope: calendar.events)
  • Your authentication token is stored temporarily in your browser session only
  • We never have access to your Google password
  • We only create calendar events - we cannot read, modify, or delete existing events
  • The connection ends when you close your browser

4. Data Processing

  • Client-Side Only: All PDF processing, shift extraction, and calendar generation happens in your browser
  • No Server Storage: We do not have any servers that store your data
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your shift data is never shared with anyone except Google Calendar (when you explicitly choose to sync)
  • No Analytics: We do not use tracking cookies or analytics services

5. Third-Party Services

This app integrates with:

  • Google Calendar API: Only when you click "Add to Google Calendar". Review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • PDF.js (Mozilla): Open-source library for PDF processing, runs entirely in your browser

6. Your Rights

Since we don't store any data, there's nothing to delete or modify on our end. You can:

  • Clear your browser's local storage to remove saved names
  • Revoke Google Calendar access at any time via your Google Account settings
  • Delete calendar events directly from Google Calendar

7. Security

Your data security is ensured by:

  • Processing everything locally in your browser
  • Using Google's secure OAuth 2.0 authentication system
  • HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • No server-side data storage or transmission

8. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for employees managing work schedules. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact
